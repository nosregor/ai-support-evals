# SunPeak Solar — Customer Support Knowledge Base

> SunPeak Solar is a fictional residential solar installer used for this learning
> project. All names, numbers and policies below are invented.

## About SunPeak Solar

SunPeak Solar designs and installs rooftop solar systems and home batteries for
homeowners. We serve customers in the (fictional) regions of Northvale and Eastmere.
We do not install systems on commercial buildings, apartment blocks or rented homes
without written consent from the property owner.

## Installation process and timelines

1. **Free consultation** (week 0): a solar advisor reviews your roof, electricity usage
   and goals by video call. You receive a written quote within 3 business days.
2. **Site survey** (weeks 1–2): a technician visits to measure the roof and inspect
   the electrical panel. The visit takes about 2 hours.
3. **Design and permits** (weeks 2–6): our engineers finalise the design and we apply
   for any local permits on your behalf. Permit times depend on your local authority.
4. **Installation day** (typically weeks 6–8): most installations take 1–2 days.
   You do not need to be home, but someone must give access to the electrical panel.
5. **Grid connection and activation** (1–3 weeks after installation): we register
   the system with your grid operator and activate monitoring in the SunPeak app.

Total time from signed contract to a working system is usually 8–12 weeks.
Weather or permit delays can extend this; your project coordinator will tell you
about any delay.

## Billing and payment plans

- **Pay in full:** 20% deposit when you sign, 80% on installation day.
- **Monthly plan:** spread the cost over 60, 120 or 180 months. The interest rate is
  shown on your personal quote. Approval is subject to a credit check by our
  financing partner.
- Invoices are sent by email and are also available in the SunPeak app under
  "Documents".
- Accepted payment methods: bank transfer and direct debit. We do not accept cash
  or credit cards.
- **Cancellation:** you can cancel free of charge within 14 days of signing. After
  that, cancellation fees depend on how far the project has progressed and are
  listed in your contract.
- Support agents cannot change prices, offer discounts or adjust payment terms.
  Questions about pricing or financing are handled by the billing team.

## Maintenance

- Solar panels need very little maintenance. Rain usually keeps them clean enough.
- We recommend a professional inspection every 2 years. You can book one in the
  SunPeak app. The cost is listed in the app at the time of booking.
- Never climb onto your roof or open the inverter yourself.
- If the SunPeak app shows a red "System offline" alert for more than 24 hours,
  contact support.
- Snow on panels melts on its own; do not try to remove it.

## Warranty basics

- **Panels:** 25-year product and performance warranty.
- **Inverter:** 10-year warranty.
- **Battery:** 10-year warranty.
- **Workmanship (installation work):** 5 years.
- Damage caused by storms, fire or other accidents is not covered by the warranty;
  this is usually covered by home insurance.
- Warranty claims are opened by the support team and reviewed by a technician.

## Account and privacy

- Support can only discuss an account with the account holder, after verifying
  their identity through the SunPeak app or by phone.
- We never share information about one customer with another person.
